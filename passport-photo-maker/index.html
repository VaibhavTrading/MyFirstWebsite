Technical Overview

Everything About Passport Photo Maker

Comprehensive guide to passport photo dimensions, background removal, tone & color calibration, and printable sheet layouts.

🪪

What This Tool Does

Passport Photo Maker is a client-side portrait studio engineered to crop, clean, retouch, and arrange passport, visa, stamp, and ID photographs directly inside your browser. It maintains a full-resolution master bitmap pipeline decoupled from physical print millimeters, supports in-browser portrait background removal with studio preset or custom backdrop colors, offers subject-only tone and RGB/CMY color balance sliders, and generates calibrated A4 and 4 × 6 inch print sheets with optional cut lines.

⭐

Main Features

  • Size Presets & Custom Units: Instant presets for 3.5 × 4.5 cm (Standard), 2 × 2 inch (US Visa), 3 × 4 cm (Stamp Size), and 2.5 × 3.5 cm (PAN / Mini), plus custom dimensions in cm, mm, inch, or px @ 300 DPI.
  • Framing & Alignment Controls: Smooth Crop Zoom (80%–250%), drag-to-pan framing with Lock Image Drag, ±90° rotation, horizontal flip, and 1-click Center Fit.
  • Background Removal & Studio Colors: Client-side portrait segmentation with Restore Original fallback, preset backdrops (Light Slate, White, Sky Blue, Beige / Cream), and a Custom Studio Color picker.
  • Tone & Color Calibration: Separate Tone & Exposure sliders (Brightness, Contrast, Midtones / Gamma 0.40–2.20) and Cyan–Red, Magenta–Green, Yellow–Blue color balance controls.
  • Print Sheet Modal & Exports: A4 and 4 × 6 inch sheet preview with Photo Size scale (-50 to +50), Photo Gap, Photo Border, optional Crop Gap & Dashed/Plain Crop Line, JPG/PDF sheet download, and Send To tool integration.
✨

Key Benefits

  • Print-Ready Physical Accuracy: Outputs exact 300 DPI pixel dimensions and calibrated millimeter spacing for A4 and 4 × 6 photo paper.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Personal portraits stay strictly inside your local browser memory without external server transmission.
  • Full-Resolution Master Quality: Small physical print sizes never downsample your live editing canvas, keeping facial details crisp.
  • Direct Portal & Studio Bridge: Send any finished passport photo directly to Image & PDF Editor for exact KB compression or Images to PDF compiling.
📁

Supported File Types & Outputs

  • Input Portraits: JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), WEBP (.webp), and browser-supported photo formats.
  • Single Photo Export: High-resolution JPEG cropped and scaled to your selected 300 DPI dimensions (e.g., 413 × 531 px for 3.5 × 4.5 cm).
  • Print Sheet Export: Calibrated A4 (210 × 297 mm) or 4 × 6 inch print-ready JPG, vector PDF sheet, and direct browser print layout.
💼

Practical Applications

  • Passport, Visa & Travel Applications: Generating 3.5 × 4.5 cm and 2 × 2 inch compliant photos on clean white or light studio backgrounds.
  • CSC / VLE & Print Shops: Packing 1 to 100 photos neatly onto A4 or 4 × 6 glossy sheets with configurable gap, border, and cut guides.
  • Exam, Job & PAN Submissions: Preparing 2.5 × 3.5 cm PAN or 3 × 4 cm stamp portraits and transferring to Image & PDF Editor for strict KB sizing.
  • Institutional ID Portraits: Standardizing uniform background tones across student badges and employee credentials.
💡

Useful Tips for Best Results

  • Use Even Front Lighting: Capture portraits facing soft daylight against a contrasting wall for the cleanest background cutout edges.
  • Lock Drag After Framing: Position head and shoulders with Crop Zoom and drag, then tick "Lock Image Drag" to prevent accidental shifts.
  • Print at 100% / Actual Size: Disable "Fit to Page" in your printer dialog so physical photo dimensions match a ruler accurately.
  • Fine-Tune Print Sheet Spacing: Use the Print Preview modal's Photo Size slider (-50 to +50), Photo Gap, and optional Crop Line toggle to maximize photos per sheet.
⚠️

Technical Limitations

  • Complex Edges & Matching Tones: Fine loose hair or clothing matching the original backdrop tone may leave minor edge halos during automatic segmentation.
  • Original Photo Sharpness: Heavily blurred or very low-resolution portraits cannot be enhanced beyond the detail present in the source file.
