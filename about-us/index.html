About SMART VLE TOOL

Free online tools for document scanning, ID card printing, passport photo making and image editing.

About SMART VLE TOOL

SMART VLE TOOL started with a simple idea based on everyday document and photo-related work.

While handling regular tasks such as ID card printing, document scanning and image compression, we realised that these small jobs often require extra time and effort. Preparing images in the correct size, cleaning scanned documents and arranging ID cards for printing can be difficult without suitable tools.

This gave us the idea to create a simple online platform where people can find useful tools for their everyday document and image requirements.

Instead of depending on different software for every small task, users can access several practical tools in one place.

Our Purpose

Our aim is to make everyday document and image-related tasks easier, quicker and more convenient.

We focus on practical features that people regularly need for printing, online applications, educational work and office requirements.

We want our tools to be simple enough for regular users, including people who may not have advanced computer knowledge.

Our Tools and Services

SMART VLE TOOL provides a practical set of browser-based utilities:

  • Document Studio: Combines three practical tools in one workspace — Document Enhancer (perspective correction, document filters, shadow removal, brightness/contrast adjustments, and A4 print preview), Images to PDF (multi-image compilation with page ordering and size limits), and PDF Optimizer (page organizing, rotation, merging, and PDF compression).
  • ID Card Studio: Helps users arrange the front and back sides of ID cards. It includes horizontal and vertical card layouts, standard card dimensions, borders, cutting guides and A4 printing options.
  • Image Editor: Provides useful features for everyday image editing, including resizing, compression, cropping, rotation, brightness and colour adjustments, format conversion and watermark addition.
  • Passport Photo Maker: Helps users prepare passport-size photographs using available photo dimensions, background removal, custom background colours, and printing layouts. Users should verify required dimensions with their specific application guidelines.
  • Other Available Tools: Includes Image to PDF, PDF Organizer (with built-in PDF compression and size optimization), Format Converter, Photo and Signature Resizer, and PDF Watermark utilities designed for everyday online applications and printing tasks.

Who Can Use SMART VLE TOOL?

Our tools are useful for:

  • Students preparing digital certificates and exam documents.
  • Job applicants formatting photographs and signatures for recruitment portals.
  • Computer operators and CSC VLEs managing daily citizen paperwork.
  • Office staff organizing and converting reports and scanned files.
  • Small businesses handling ID cards and official documents.
  • Anyone who regularly works with photographs and digital documents.

Our Approach

We believe everyday digital work should not be unnecessarily complicated.

Our focus is on providing useful features in a simple interface so visitors can complete common document and photo-related tasks without struggling with complicated software.

We continue to improve the website and welcome genuine suggestions and feedback from users.
